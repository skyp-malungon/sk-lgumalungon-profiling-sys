The Data Journey

A complete workflow example of the SK Youth Profiling System from paper to dashboard.

Meet Juan. To understand how the system works, let's follow the journey of Juan Dela Cruz, a 19-year-old out-of-school youth from Barangay Banate, Malungon. Watch how his data moves through the 4 steps of the system.

Step 1

Generate Forms

The SK Secretary opens the Form Generator to create official, serialized forms specifically for their barangay before heading to the field.

The Scenario

The SK Secretary selects "Banate" and Year "2025". They print 50 forms. The system generates unique Control Numbers for every single page.

Barangay: Banate
Control No: SK-Mal-05-25-00001
Step 2

Collect & Encode

Enumerators visit households. The youth fill out the physical paper forms (and sign the data privacy consent). The enumerator then quickly encodes this into the offline Android App using standardized codes.

The Scenario

Juan fills out Form #00001. Enumerator Maria takes the paper and encodes Juan's answers into the Android App. To save time and prevent typos, the app uses shortcodes.

Name: Juan Dela Cruz
Tribe Input: BLA (stands for Blaan)
Employment: U (stands for Unemployed)
Sector: OSY (Out-of-School Youth)
Step 3

Upload to Cloud

Once profiling is done, the enumerator exports a CSV file from the Android App and uploads it via the web CSV Uploader. The system automatically translates all the shortcodes back to readable text.

The Scenario

Maria exports a file named SK_Survey_Banate.csv and drops it into the Uploader. The system reads the code "BLA" and saves it to the central database as "Blaan".

Upload Successful!
Barangay: Banate
Records Appended: 50
(Including Juan Dela Cruz)
Step 4

Analyze & Act

The SK Chairperson logs into the SKYMS Dashboard. The database is instantly updated. They can filter data, view charts, and print official Master Lists for specific programs.

The Scenario

The SK is planning an Alternative Learning System (ALS) program. The Chairperson filters the dashboard:

  • Barangay: Banate
  • Youth Sector: Out-of-School Youth

Juan Dela Cruz appears on the Master List. The SK prints the report and reaches out to Juan to enroll him in the program. Data-driven governance in action!

Process Complete

From a blank piece of paper to a life-changing youth program.
