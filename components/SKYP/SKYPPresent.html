SK Youth Profiling System

Empowering youth governance through data-driven decisions.

Why do we need this system?

Targeted Programs
Stop guessing. We need to know exactly what the youth need (e.g., jobs, education, sports) to allocate the SK budget effectively.
Centralized Registry
Move away from scattered papers. Maintain a secure, centralized database of all Katipunan ng Kabataan (KK) members.
Efficiency
Automated age calculation, standardized demographics, and real-time generation of municipal and barangay-level reports.
Data Privacy
Compliant with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act). Secure handling of sensitive youth profiles and demographics.

The System Workflow

A seamless connection from groundwork to executive dashboards.

Dashboard & Reports
Step 1

Form Generator

We start by generating serialized, trackable physical forms for the enumerators.

  • Unique QR Codes & Control Numbers (e.g., SK-Mal-01-25-00001) track every single paper form.
  • Barangay Specific: Select the Barangay and Year, and the system customizes the forms instantly.
  • Batch Printing: Generate 50, 100, or 500 forms at once, perfectly formatted for standard legal paper.
Step 2

Data Collection (Groundwork)

Enumerators head to the field to profile the youth.

Paper Profiling
Using the generated forms, enumerators capture critical data: Education, Employment, Youth Sector, Income, and specifically, Tribal Affiliation and Languages Spoken.
Android App Encoding
Data is entered into the customized offline Android App. The app uses strict drop-downs (to prevent typos) and exports a clean .csv file when finished.
Step 3

Cloud Uploader

Moving the data from the Android device to the Central Database.

  • Drag & Drop CSV: Simply upload the file exported from the app (e.g., SK_Survey_Banate.csv).
  • Auto-Decoding: The system automatically translates app codes (e.g., "M" → "Male", "BLA" → "Blaan") into readable formats.
  • Digital Receipts: Generates an upload receipt PDF showing the timestamp, barangay, and total respondents appended.
Step 4

Management Dashboard (SKYMS)

The command center for SK Officials and Local Youth Development Officers.

Live Filtering
Instantly sort by Barangay, Sex, Tribe, or Eligibility (15-30 vs Over-aged).
Visual Analytics
Auto-generated charts for Employment, Education, and Youth Sector distribution.
Youth Registry
A searchable master list. Click any name to view their complete individual profile.
1-Click Reports
Generate print-ready Master Lists and Statistical Summaries with official letterheads.

A Note on Data Privacy

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

As enumerators and officials, you are handling sensitive personal information (income, civil status, contact details).

  • Never share raw data or CSV files outside the official system.
  • Ensure respondents sign the Data Privacy Consent block on the paper form.
  • Data is used strictly for youth development planning and policy formulation.
Your Role

Action Plan for Enumerators

  • Claim Your Forms: Get your batch of serialized forms from the SK Secretary.
  • Conduct Profiling: Ensure forms are filled out legibly and completely, especially the consent signature.
  • Encode Accurately: Transfer data carefully to the Android App to ensure our charts and reports are 100% accurate.
  • Submit: Export the CSV and assist in uploading it via the cloud portal.
